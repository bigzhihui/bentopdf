Privacy

How this site handles your data

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This page describes pdf.afeiii.com, operated under the name Thirteen Months PDF Tools. For questions, email support@afeiii.com.

Documents you select

The PDF tools are designed to read and process selected documents in your browser. The site does not offer an account or a document storage service. Some tools load processing code, fonts or OCR language data over the network. Features that open a web address, validate a digital signature or access another online resource can make network requests; review the specific tool and your browser's network activity when handling sensitive material.

Site hosting and analytics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Page requests pass through Cloudflare. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page views and performance. Its browser beacon sends site usage and performance measurements to Cloudflare; it does not upload the PDF documents selected in the tools. Cloudflare describes its data collection and privacy approach in its documentation.

Local settings and third parties

Your browser may store language and interface preferences and cache application files for faster loading. When you open a third-party link, or when a tool fetches an external component or URL, the relevant provider receives that network request. Their own privacy terms apply.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, contact support@afeiii.com. Please do not send sensitive PDF documents unless they are necessary for a support request and you are comfortable sharing them.

隐私说明

本站如何处理您的数据

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日。本说明适用于以“十三月 PDF 工具”名义运营的 pdf.afeiii.com。如有疑问，请联系 support@afeiii.com。

您选择的文件

本站 PDF 工具设计为在浏览器中读取并处理您选择的文件；本站不提供账号或文件托管服务。部分工具会从网络下载处理组件、字体或 OCR 语言数据。打开网页地址、验证数字签名或访问其他在线资源的功能可能产生网络请求。处理敏感材料时，请查看具体工具的行为及浏览器网络请求。

网站托管与访问统计

本站托管于 Cloudflare Pages，页面访问请求会经过 Cloudflare。本站使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 了解页面访问与性能情况；其浏览器脚本会向 Cloudflare 发送网站使用和性能指标，不会上传您在工具中选择的 PDF 文件。Cloudflare 对数据收集与隐私处理有公开说明。

本地设置与第三方

浏览器可能保存语言、界面偏好，并缓存应用文件以便后续加载。打开第三方链接，或使用需要获取外部组件、网页地址的工具时，相关服务提供方会收到对应网络请求，并适用其自身的隐私规则。

联系我们

如有隐私方面的问题，请发送邮件至 support@afeiii.com。除非排查问题确有必要且您愿意分享，请勿通过邮件发送敏感 PDF 文件。
